Start here: what this notebook is

18 October 2026 · 1 min read · meta

This is a working notebook on neutrino physics, particle detectors and computational astroparticle physics, written by an undergraduate at IISc while the subject is still being learned. It is not a textbook and not a collection of finished answers.

What is covered

Most posts start from one question that came up during real work: why a calculated event rate disagreed with a published table, why a sensitivity forecast changes when the supernova model changes, what a gravitational-wave signal looks like before the noise is removed. Each post tries to answer that single question, with one figure and the physics needed to follow it.

The first few posts walk through the projects listed on the main site: how supernova neutrinos reach a detector, how event rates are calculated, and how published results were reproduced from scratch.

How posts are written

Every post ends with a section called "What is still unclear". A reproduced number does not validate every assumption behind it, and a post that claims more than the calculation shows would defeat the purpose of the notebook. Where a result did not match a published value, the mismatch is reported as found.

What will not appear

Unpublished results, group code and anything belonging to collaborators stay out until they are cleared. Posts about ongoing research appear only after the supervisor has seen them.

Corrections are welcome by email, and errors will be fixed in the open with a dated note.
