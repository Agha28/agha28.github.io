## 📂 What's Inside the App?

Once you open the app, you will see these main sections on the left side menu:

### 🪖 Squad Roles

Choose your soldier type: Rifleman, Medic, Scout, or Machine Gunner. Each role has different abilities. The app shows you a description of each role and what your job is on the battlefield.

### 🔫 Weapon Loadouts

Browse all available weapons from the game. For each weapon, you will see:
- Damage rating
- Effective range
- Reload speed
- Recommended attachments

You can save your favorite loadouts for quick access before a battle.

### 🎯 Manual Aim Practice

This is a built-in training mode. A moving target appears on your screen. You click to shoot. The app tracks your accuracy percentage and reaction time. Practice here to improve your real-game performance.

### 🗺️ Map Planning

Open any battlefield map. You can draw lines, place markers, and add notes directly on the map. Plan your squad's route before you jump into the action. Your plans are saved automatically on your computer.

### 📊 Frontline Battle Analytics

After each match, enter your results (kills, deaths, objectives captured). The app creates simple charts showing your progress over time. See if you are improving week by week.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Problems

### Problem: The app won't open after double-clicking.

- Make sure you extracted the files. Do not run the app from inside the `.zip` file.
- Try right-clicking the app and selecting "Run as administrator."
- Check your antivirus program. It may be blocking the app. Add an exception for `FrontlineToolkit.exe`.

### Problem: The map images are blank.

- Close the app and reopen it.
- Make sure you have an internet connection the first time you open a map.

### Problem: The text is too small or too large.

- Use the slider at the bottom right corner of the app window to adjust text size.

### Problem: I forgot my saved loadouts.

- Look for a file named `loadouts.json` in the `FrontlineData` folder. If you deleted it, your loadouts are gone. But you can recreate them easily.
